JOB DESCRIPTION

Job Title Junior AI/ML Engineer

Company Name NovaMind Analytics Pvt. Ltd.

Job Summary NovaMind Analytics is looking for a Junior AI/ML Engineer to
join its applied artificial intelligence team. The role is designed for
an early-career candidate who has a strong foundation in Python, machine
learning concepts, data structures, and problem solving, and who can
turn academic knowledge into working technical solutions. The selected
candidate will work with senior engineers and data scientists on data
preparation, model development, evaluation, experimentation,
documentation, and basic deployment activities. The position is suitable
for a recent graduate or final-year undergraduate who can demonstrate
practical evidence through academic projects, internships, coursework,
competitions, or other technical work.

The ideal candidate should be curious, organized, comfortable learning
new tools, and able to communicate technical ideas clearly. Academic
grades are considered, but demonstrated evidence of practical ability is
more important than grades alone.

Must-Have Skills The candidate must have practical working knowledge of
Python and a basic understanding of machine learning workflows. The
candidate must understand fundamental data structures and algorithms and
be comfortable with problem solving. The candidate must have experience
using common Python data libraries such as NumPy and pandas and should
understand basic model evaluation concepts. The candidate must be able
to work with structured datasets, perform basic data cleaning, and
explain the reasoning behind a technical solution. The candidate must
have at least one completed project or equivalent evidence demonstrating
practical programming or machine learning work.

Preferred Skills Experience with scikit-learn, Jupyter Notebook,
Git/GitHub, SQL, data visualization, or a cloud platform is preferred.
Exposure to deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision,
APIs, model deployment, or MLOps is an advantage. Participation in
hackathons, technical competitions, research work, internships,
open-source contributions, or student technology clubs is also valued.

Minimum Experience A minimum of 0 to 1 year of relevant practical
experience is required. Relevant academic projects, internships,
freelance technical work, research assistance, or substantial personal
projects may be considered as evidence for an entry-level applicant.

Education Requirements The candidate should be pursuing or have
completed a bachelor’s degree in Artificial Intelligence, Machine
Learning, Computer Science, Information Technology, Data Science,
Electronics, or a closely related discipline. Candidates in the final
two years of an undergraduate program may apply. A consistent academic
record is preferred.

Certifications No certification is mandatory. A recognized certification
in Python, machine learning, data analytics, cloud computing, or a
related technical area is preferred when supported by practical
evidence.

Location and Work Mode Location: Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. Work mode:
Hybrid, with three days per week expected from the Mumbai office.
Candidates must be able to work from Mumbai on the required office days.

Responsibilities The selected candidate will prepare and clean datasets,
assist in exploratory data analysis, implement and test machine learning
models, compare model performance using suitable evaluation metrics,
write readable and maintainable Python code, document experiments and
results, collaborate with senior engineers and analysts, troubleshoot
basic technical issues, maintain project files and version-controlled
code, and present project findings in a clear manner. The candidate may
also assist with simple model-serving APIs, dashboards, data pipelines,
or deployment tasks depending on project requirements.

Constraints and Deal Breakers Applicants who cannot demonstrate basic
Python programming ability will not be considered. Applicants with no
evidence of completing a technical project, internship, research
assignment, or equivalent practical work will be treated as weak
matches. Candidates who cannot meet the Mumbai hybrid attendance
requirement are not eligible. Misrepresentation of academic records,
work experience, projects, certifications, or technical skills is a deal
breaker. Candidates who claim advanced machine learning expertise
without evidence of implementation, evaluation, or documented results
may be rejected. Strong communication and teamwork are expected;
repeated inability to explain one’s own work is a concern.

Evidence Standard Every important candidate claim should be supported by
a concrete source of evidence such as a project name and outcome,
internship responsibility, GitHub repository, competition result,
certification, academic coursework, measurable improvement, published
work, or clearly described implementation. Evidence should be specific
enough for a recruiter to verify the claim rather than relying on
generic statements such as ‘good at Python’ or ‘hardworking’.

Selection Focus Candidates will be evaluated primarily on evidence of
Python and machine learning fundamentals, quality and relevance of
projects, ability to explain technical decisions, academic foundation,
practical exposure, communication, and fit with the Mumbai hybrid
requirement. The role is entry-level, so potential and demonstrated
learning ability are considered alongside existing experience.
